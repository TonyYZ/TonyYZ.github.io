Yutong Zhou

M2 Cognitive Science student at ENS-PSL

I am interested in grounded conceptual representation, computational models of meaning and semantic generalization, spatial and event concepts, lexical semantic extension, colexification, semantic typology, and language and embodied cognition.

About

I am currently an M2 student in Cognitive Science at ENS-PSL, after completing a B.A. in Cognitive Science with minors in Computer Science and Media Arts and Design at the University of Chicago. My research focuses on how structured conceptual representations support generalization and linguistic meaning. I combine behavioral experiments, computational modeling, and multimodal semantic analysis.

Selected Research

Embodied LoT

  • Developed a probabilistic concept-learning model using a compositional Language of Thought and Metropolis–Hastings inference over structured visual events.
  • Implemented program generation, event rendering, inference, and visualization tools.

GitHub

Human and Vision-Language Model Reasoning over Multimodal Meaning

  • Current research at LSCP / Institut Jean Nicod with Yair Lakretz and Philippe Schlenker.
  • Comparing human and vision-language model reasoning over semantic and pragmatic inferences conveyed through animations, gestures, and emoji.
  • Building pipelines for stimulus processing, model prompting, and quantitative analysis.

Mandarin Sentence Production under Simulated Contact with English

  • Research with Ming Xiang at the University of Chicago.
  • Studying how English exposure influences Mandarin sentence production in bilingual speakers.

Manuscript in preparation.

Infant Phonological Discrimination

  • Research with Sho Tsuji at LSCP.
  • Developed an asynchronous online paradigm on Lookit for infant phonological discrimination.
  • Built a gaze-data preprocessing and analysis pipeline for webcam-based looking-time data.

Other Projects
